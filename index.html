Ant Almanac

28 ant species from the Teri dune landscape, Tamil Nadu — imaged for morphological trait measurements as part of my MSc thesis understanding how habitat shape ant assemblage.

Tap any image to zoom — each photograph includes a 1 mm scale bar.

These images are shared for viewing purposes only and are published on trust. Any form of sharing, reuse, or publishing requires prior contact with the study's authors.
